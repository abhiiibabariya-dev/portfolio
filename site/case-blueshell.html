BlueShell
Threat Intelligence & Blue Team Operations.

An open-source security operations platform combining detection content, training resources and automated threat intelligence workflows.

Overview

BlueShell is documented as a hacker-themed blue-team operations platform with detection rules, training material, automated threat intelligence tooling and security operations resources.

Key Features

  • Detection rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK across multiple SIEM platforms.
  • Training and operational resources for SIEM, EDR, XDR and SOAR workflows.
  • Automated threat intelligence collection and rule-generation concepts.
  • Interactive security-focused web interface and terminal-style experience.
